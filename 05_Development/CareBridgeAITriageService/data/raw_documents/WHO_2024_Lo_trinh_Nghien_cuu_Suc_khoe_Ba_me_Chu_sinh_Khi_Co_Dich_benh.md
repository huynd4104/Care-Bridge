Box 1. Main findings of the landscape analysis on maternal and perinatal
health research in the context of epidemic threats

Finding 1: substantial research efforts exist; there is sufficient infrastructure to support
robust maternal and perinatal health research during epidemics mainly in high-income
settings. There is a broad geographical distribution of research efforts, but a more
significant presence from Europe and North America. Multi-country networks enhance
access to diverse study populations, promoting the generalizability of findings and enabling
prompt dissemination of studies results and interventions across diverse populations.

Finding 2: the existing infrastructure is best suited to providing epidemiological data;
research and development (R&D) including pregnant women during epidemics is
